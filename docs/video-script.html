Part 1 — User Flow (55s)

[Screen: user-flow.html open]

"This is iLumos — an AI chat interface for refining patent claim charts. Setup is three quick steps: upload the claim chart, optionally add reference docs the AI can cite as evidence, then set system instructions that guide its tone — for example, being conservative about legal claims.

From there it's a loop: the analyst picks a claim element, asks the AI in chat to refine it — strengthen evidence, fix vague reasoning, whatever's needed — and the AI proposes a specific change. Nothing touches the chart until the analyst accepts, rejects, or modifies it. That loop repeats element by element until the chart's ready to export.

I also built in three required edge cases: if the AI cites the wrong evidence, the analyst corrects it right in chat. If they disagree with an accepted change, they can undo it. And if the AI genuinely can't find supporting evidence, instead of guessing, it asks the analyst to upload a document."
