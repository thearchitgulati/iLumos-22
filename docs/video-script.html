Part 1 — User Flow (60s)

[Screen: user-flow.html open]

"This is iLumos — an AI chat interface for refining patent claim charts. The flow starts with the analyst uploading a claim chart and optional product docs, then setting system instructions that guide the AI's tone — for example, being conservative about legal claims.

From there it's a loop: the analyst picks a claim element, asks the AI in chat to refine it — strengthen evidence, fix vague reasoning, whatever's needed — and the AI proposes a specific change. Nothing touches the chart until the analyst accepts, rejects, or modifies it. That loop repeats element by element until the chart's ready to export.

I also built in three edge cases: if the AI cites the wrong evidence, the analyst corrects it right in chat. If the analyst disagrees with an accepted change, they can undo it. And if the AI genuinely can't find supporting evidence, instead of guessing, it asks the analyst to upload a document or paste a URL."
